# Dungeons on Automatic Monster Library 0.3.2

A data-correctness release. 175 trait statements that the authorized source states were missing from the
published records; all of them are restored, and eight records are repriced as a result.

Original GURPS conversion by Enraged Eggplant, from *Monsters* (May 11, 2024); adapted and republished with
permission. Released under [CC BY 4.0](../../licenses/CC-BY-4.0.txt) with mandatory originator credit.

## What was wrong

The authorized source states each creature's traits **twice**: once in the racial template
(`**Advantages:**` and its companion lines) and again as a `**Traits:**` summary inside the Typical Stats
block. The JSON conversion reads the summary. The GCS conversion reads the template.

Wherever the summary was incomplete, the published record and the `.gct` for the same creature disagreed —
and nothing downstream could tell. The published `.gct` and `.gcs` downloads were correct throughout; the
JSON stat block was the artifact that was wrong.

Androsphinx is representative:

```
**Advantages:** ... Flight (Cannot Hover, -15%; Winged, -25%) [24] ...
**Traits:** Enhanced Move 1 (Air); Extended Lifespan 2; Extra Legs (Four Legs); Fur; ...
```

`Enhanced Move (Air)` survived; `Flight` did not. Ten records were left carrying a modifier that multiplies
an air Move only Flight can provide.

## What changed

**33 records regained Flight** ([#26](https://github.com/Zuljita/DungeonsOnAutomaticMonsters/issues/26),
`review/repairs/004-missing-flight.json`). Six of them fly without wings — the four genies, Bralani and
Ghaele — so no check against the artwork would have found them.

**88 records regained 142 further trait statements**
([#29](https://github.com/Zuljita/DungeonsOnAutomaticMonsters/issues/29),
`review/repairs/005-missing-traits.json`): Not Subject to Fatigue, Tunneling, Mute, Constriction Attack,
Regeneration, Regrowth, Extra Head, Supernatural Durability and others. Every one was already present in the
corresponding `.gct`.

All restorations come from the racial template with the modifiers the source states. Costs are dropped
because record traits state modifiers, not prices.

## Ratings changed on eight records

`scripts/review/cer.mjs` reads four traits: high pain threshold, recovery, combat reflexes, unfazeable. Two
were among the missing, so eight records were rated from an incomplete trait list. They are now repriced:

to 0.3.1.

## Guard against recurrence

`npm run review:audit-traits` compares the racial template against everything the published record expresses
— traits, attacks, skills and notes — and reports what the template states and the record does not. It runs
as part of `npm test` and currently reports **0 divergence across 304 records**.

The audit needs the source corpus under the ignored `data/` directory, so on a clean checkout it reports
"skipped" rather than failing. Its report is tracked at `review/reports/trait-source-audit.md`.

It deliberately excludes three classes: natural weapons purchased as advantages but published as attacks;
attribute-like entries published as `stats.attributes`; and source misspellings the record already carries
(`Resitricted Diet`, `infraivision`, `odious racial habie`, `nigh vision`, `discirminatory smell`,
`constriciton attack`, `dobule-jointed`, `odious radial habit`, `doesn't breath`). Two trait names in wide
use are never collapsed into each other, so Clinging and Climbing stay distinct.

## Also in this release

The package and its source-book toggle now present as **Dungeons on Automatic Monster Library**
([#27](https://github.com/Zuljita/DungeonsOnAutomaticMonsters/pull/27)). The source-book `id` is unchanged,
so existing saves keep this library enabled, and every originator credit is untouched.

## Known inconsistency

The four sphinxes now state `Flight (Cannot Hover; Winged)`, but their generated portraits and tokens show no
wings, and their 0.3.1 descriptions are silent on flight because they were written against the pre-repair
record. Neither is wrong as data; they no longer describe quite the same creature. Regenerating that art and
revisiting those four descriptions is open work.
